LIST:FILTER¶

This generator expression belongs to the List Transformations family. See that section for related generator expressions.

Syntax¶

$<LIST:FILTER,list,INCLUDE|EXCLUDE,regex>

Added in version 3.27.

A list of items from the list which match (INCLUDE) or do not match (EXCLUDE) the regular expression regex. The result is the same as $<FILTER:list,INCLUDE|EXCLUDE,regex>.

Added in version 4.5: The regular expression may be introduced explicitly with a REGEX keyword, and a PREDICATE keyword selects items using a generator expression instead:

$<LIST:FILTER,list,INCLUDE|EXCLUDE,REGEX,regex>
$<LIST:FILTER,list,INCLUDE|EXCLUDE,PREDICATE,body>

With PREDICATE, body is evaluated once per item with the bound operand $<_0> expanding to the current item. The body must evaluate to exactly 0 or 1; INCLUDE keeps items whose body yields 1 and EXCLUDE removes them. Use $<BOOL:...> to coerce other values. Because REGEX and PREDICATE are now keywords, a bare regular expression equal to REGEX or PREDICATE must use the explicit REGEX form.
