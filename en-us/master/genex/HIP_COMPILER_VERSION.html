HIP_COMPILER_VERSION¶

This generator expression belongs to the Compiler Version family. See that section for related generator expressions.

Syntax¶

$<HIP_COMPILER_VERSION>

Added in version 3.21.

The version of the HIP compiler used.

Syntax¶

$<HIP_COMPILER_VERSION:version>

Added in version 3.21.

1 if the version of the HIP compiler matches version, otherwise 0.
