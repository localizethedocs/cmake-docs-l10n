fastbuild-cache-plugin¶

  • The FASTBuild generator gained the CMAKE_FASTBUILD_CACHE_PLUGIN and CMAKE_FASTBUILD_CACHE_PLUGIN_CONFIG variables to use a FASTBuild cache plugin, and the CMAKE_FASTBUILD_CACHE_MODE variable to enable caching in builds started by cmake --build.
